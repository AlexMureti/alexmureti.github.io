About

I'm a software and AI engineer in Nairobi. At Brisk Credit, a licensed Kenyan lender, I built and run the credit-scoring API that a partner bank posts to in production, and the website that writes loan applications straight into the lender's loan-management system across 42 branches.

Before and alongside that, I've shipped sites and systems for a herbs exporter, an urban-farming enterprise, a short-stay apartment, a dealership, a pharmacy, a church and a disability-advocacy organisation. Most of them take orders over WhatsApp and payments over M-Pesa, because that is what their customers actually use.

I also make the films. Since September I've cut forty-three short films for five of these brands, using AI for voice, music and inserts and real footage for everything a viewer would recognise as real. Outside work I write songs.

Implementation and sales partner for software companies entering Kenya and East Africa: local integrations, rollout, first-line support, and the launch films. alexmkmaingi@gmail.com.
