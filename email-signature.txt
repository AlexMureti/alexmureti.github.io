------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE ON THE "--" LINE
------------------------------------------------------------------
The two hyphens and a space on their own line is the RFC 3676
signature delimiter. Mail clients that understand it will collapse
or grey out everything below automatically, and will strip it from
quoted replies so your signature does not stack up down a thread.

It costs nothing and almost nobody uses it.
